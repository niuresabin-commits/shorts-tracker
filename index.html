How your week works

The pipeline
  • Find clips any time. Paste the link into Clips right away.
  • Make days (Sat, Sun, Mon, Tue). Make today's Short. On Sat, Sun and Mon also make one extra, set its publish date to Wed, Thu and Fri, and schedule it in YouTube Studio.
  • College days (Wed to Fri). You make nothing. The Short you scheduled goes live and you tick it off.
  • 48 hours after a Short goes live the app asks for its numbers. Always record at 48 hours so every Short is judged at the same age.
  • 7 days after you get a small check to see how it is still doing. It never changes the 48-hour numbers.
What a test is

You post every day anyway. A test is a rule for how you make those Shorts, so the results can be compared fairly. For example: this week, five Shorts in a row open with a question. Next, five open with a bold claim. After that you see which group did better.

Nothing extra to do. You pick the option when you save a Short, and the app counts to five and moves to the next test for you. Movie clips differ a lot, so trust a result only after 3 or more Shorts per option.
